Privacy Policy

Pixel Foundry Poster · Effective November 12, 2026

This policy explains what Pixel Foundry Poster, the tool Pixel Foundry uses to post videos of its Roblox games to its own TikTok account, collects and how it is used.

01What we collect

Only data from Pixel Foundry's own TikTok account, through TikTok's official APIs:

  • Basic profile details (account ID, display name, avatar) so the app knows which account is signed in.
  • The account's own public videos and their view, like, comment and share counts.
  • Sign-in tokens that TikTok issues so the app can post.

We do not collect data about other TikTok users, viewers or commenters, and we do not use cookies or trackers on this page.

02How we use it

  • To post gameplay videos of our games to our own account.
  • To show us how those videos perform, so we can plan better ones.

03Where it is kept

Sign-in tokens and video stats are stored on Pixel Foundry's own computer. A summary of video stats is kept in a private dashboard that only Pixel Foundry can open.

04Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone. It is sent only to TikTok, when the app talks to TikTok's API.

05Keeping and deleting data

We keep video stats for as long as they help us plan content. Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions. When access is removed, we delete the stored sign-in tokens.

06Children

The app is used only by Pixel Foundry and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

07Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

08Contact

Questions or requests: send a direct message to the Pixel Foundry account on TikTok. We aim to reply within 7 days.
